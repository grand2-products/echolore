## Meetings basics

Video, audio, and screen sharing powered by LiveKit.

- Start instant meetings and share invite links
- Guest participation via invite-token URLs
- Screen sharing and per-device camera/mic control

## Recording

Server-side recording via LiveKit Egress (8 GB RAM recommended). Recordings are stored through your configured storage provider (local / S3 / GCS).

## AI minutes

With an LLM configured, the AI agent generates summaries from meeting transcripts (Google Cloud Speech) and saves them to the Wiki.

- Real-time transcription during meetings
- Post-meeting summary generation and Wiki page creation
- Meeting AI agents (defined in the admin console) support explicit invocation and optional autonomous participation

## Calendar

Schedule meetings and join at the scheduled time.

## Coworking

A persistent coworking room (HLS streaming) is also available. Video quality is configurable in the admin console.
